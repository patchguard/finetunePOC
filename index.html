Mission:
Blue Marble

You're the Mission Director of a young space agency, and Earth is counting on you. Design satellites that find where methane leaks are worst — oil and gas fields, farms, landfills, thawing permafrost — fly them in a simulated, warming world, and use the feedback to build a better one.
